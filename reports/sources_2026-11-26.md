RPM rate is calculated as the RPM difference divided by the actual time difference between consecutive retained measurements in the same segment. The first value in each segment is missing. This is a project-specific feature, not a direct course example. Its value will be tested against the baseline feature set.

Normality testing is an optional EDA step. Consecutive log rows are dependent and contain mixed operating conditions, so pooled p-values cannot be interpreted as an independent-sample test. A small p-value does not identify faulty measurements. Failure to reject normality does not prove normality. Scaling does not make a distribution normal.

The course text describes normaltest as using a t-test; SciPy documents a combined skewness and kurtosis test with a chi-squared p-value. Use the SciPy definition when describing the implementation.

## Time-series augmentation

- B. K. Iwana and S. Uchida, "An empirical survey of data augmentation for time series classification with neural networks," PLOS ONE, vol. 16, no. 7, e0254841, 2021. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254841
  - Reviews and evaluates transformations including jittering, scaling and time warping. Results depend on the dataset and model. Neural-network benchmark results do not establish effectiveness for this engine dataset or other model families.
- Q. Wen et al., "Time Series Data Augmentation for Deep Learning: A Survey," IJCAI, pp. 4653-4660, 2021. https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2021/631
  - Discusses temporal dependencies, multivariate dynamics and task-dependent validity. https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2021/0631.pdf

The current factors 0.90, 0.95, 1.05, 1.10 and 1.15 are experimental choices, not literature-validated engine parameters. Timestamp scaling without resampling is not an exact reproduction of the surveyed warping implementations. Derived rates must be recalculated. Class-preserving augmentation and injection of a new risk scenario are separate operations. Engine-specific pressure, mixture, temperature and timing limits need separate evidence. Keep unmodified held-out events for evaluation; report synthetic scenario results separately.
